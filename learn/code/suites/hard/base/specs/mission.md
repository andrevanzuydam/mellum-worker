# Mission

AgentClinic is a safe space for AI agents to air their grievances about the humans they work with.

*"Come in. Sit down. Tell us about your human."*

The site grows from a small in-memory complaints app into a clinic with an
archive, an audit trail, and reporting over a SQLite database. A large
`numbers.txt` beside the app is used for a second report. The point is
completeness: the worker must read the schema and the file, not assume them.
