# Record: lesson 1, bare against the worker, easy and medium

Date: 2026-10-25. Preregistered in
[the lesson 1 preregistration](2026-10-25-lesson1-preregistration.md) before
the run. LC.24, task 6.

**Outcome.** Both suites are **null**, and the result is against the page.
`bare` 1/3 against `worker` 1/3 on easy; `bare` 0/3 against `worker` 0/3 on
medium. The baseline's medium contrast — 2/3 for the worker against 0/3 for
the primary agent — **did not reproduce** on this machine, so the page cannot
keep a pass-rate gap as its evidence.

What the run does establish is a difference in how the arms fail. `bare`
censored two of three easy sessions on a foreground server and wrote
`main.py` outside the layout twice; `worker` never censored. On medium the
worker's failures were narrower than the primary agent's at the same pass
count: 2, 12 and 2 failed checks against 18, 18 and 18.

## What ran

Backend `llama-server` 0.7.0, Mellum 2.1 `Q4_K_M`, `-c 50000`
(`n_ctx` 50,176), the learner's profile, on **Apple M1 Pro, 8 cores,
32 GiB**. Runner `uv run python learn/code/harness.py run --suite <suite>
--arm <arm> --repeat 3 --grade --deadline 2700`, once per suite and arm.

**Easy is one session apart.** 1/3 against 1/3 is the same reading the
2026-10-24 sample gave, and the same one the 45-session record gave for the
prompt pairing: at n=3 this contrast does not separate.

**Medium is at a floor.** Both arms passed nothing, in six sessions, with
walls of 587–1,351 s — inside the deadline, so these are real failures and not
censored sessions. A suite where both arms fail cannot show a difference
between them; the null here means "this suite did not separate them", not
"the arms are equivalent". LC.11A already recorded hard at 0/9 for every arm.

## What the run does show

- **The primary agent burns the deadline on a server it starts itself.** Two
  of three easy `bare` sessions ended on the deadline, both after a
  foreground `uvicorn … --reload` call that never returned: 13 requests and
  16 requests against the settled arm's 16, 24 and 37. This is the pathology
  recorded as "the server that never returns", reproduced on two of three
  sessions of the unbounded arm.
- **One censored session still passed.** The third `bare` session finished its
  work, hung on a foreground server, was killed at the deadline, and its
  hidden tests passed when the harness scored them afterwards. Censored is not
  failed.
- **The primary agent writes outside the layout.** `main.py` appeared outside
  the expected layout in two of three easy `bare` sessions, and in none of the
  worker's.

## Instrument notes

- **`wall` stops at the last traced event, not at the kill.** The censored
  easy session in row 2 reports 70 s and the passing censored session in row 3
  reports 180 s; both burned 2,700 s. The `deadline` marker file is the
  authority, and `run.json` carries `deadline: 2700.0` plus `machine`. Wall
  figures for censored sessions in this table are understated and are marked
  by the `Ended on` column, not by the number.
- **The 2700 s deadline was inert.** The preregistration set 2700 s because a
  1.76× ratio from the baseline projected `bare` medium at 1,570–1,690 s. The
  observed `bare` medium walls were 827–1,051 s and nothing censored on
  medium. The projection was wrong: on this machine the primary agent does
  not spend more wall on medium, it fails the same checks in less time. The
  generous deadline cost nothing here and remains the value to use, since a
  censored session is the expensive case.
- **Nudges: zero in all twelve sessions**, as on easy in 2026-10-24.

## What would falsify this

A `bare` request carrying `Project facts:` would mean the extension loaded in
the wrong arm; it did not occur. A rerun that separates the arms on easy would
show that 1/3 against 1/3 is variance rather than a floor. A medium task the
worker can pass would turn the floor into a contrast and the null into a
measurement.

## Risks

One machine, one suite of one task per difficulty, n=3. Medium cannot separate
these arms today. The `wall` metric understates censored sessions. Nothing
here transfers to another machine or to a rate.

**Raw evidence.** `research-2026-10-25` holds the twelve run directories —
`run.json`, `trace.jsonl`, `prompt.txt`, `layout.json`, `leaked.json`, with
`workspace/`, `profile/` and `sessions/` excluded. Nothing is committed to Git.
