## Extraction policy

**PR #1 is never merged.** It is a source of patches.

Each useful part is extracted onto a branch from current `main`, with only that
part and its tests. It is then:

- **kept** — landed on its own branch, with evidence; no draft PR;
- **deferred** — recorded here with the condition that would reopen it;
- **dropped** — recorded here with the reason, and not carried into code.

A commit body names its source, for example `Source: PR #1 5004187`. PR #1
stays open after every part has a decision. The digest ends with a comment on
the PR; it is never closed or merged.
